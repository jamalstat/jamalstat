# Dr Jamal Hossain

**Applied Health Statistician | Lecturer in Applied Health Statistics**  
School of Health Sciences, University of Southampton

I am an applied health statistician working across health research, statistical methodology, data science and quantitative research methods. My work includes the design and analysis of clinical and health studies, statistical modelling, prediction, longitudinal analysis, and methodological support for multidisciplinary research.

## Statistical and research interests

- Applied health statistics and biostatistics
- Regression and multilevel modelling
- Longitudinal and repeated-measures analysis
- Survival analysis
- Diagnostic accuracy and prediction modelling
- Causal inference and observational studies
- Statistical research methods
- Data science and reproducible analysis
- Statistics education

## HealthStats Lab

I am developing **HealthStats Lab**, an interactive statistics learning platform for health sciences students, postgraduate researchers and applied health researchers.

📊 [Explore HealthStats Lab](https://jamalstat.github.io/healthstats-lab/)

### Current interactive resource

## Teaching and research

My teaching focuses on applied quantitative research methods, statistics for health sciences, interpretation of statistical evidence, and supporting MSc and PhD researchers with quantitative analysis.

I am particularly interested in making statistical methods accessible while maintaining methodological rigour.
